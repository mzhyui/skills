Follow the scientific-figure skill. Keep scientific abstraction, graphical composition, rendering checks, visual review, and scientific fidelity as separate evidence categories. Preserve supplied sources and disclose reconstructed content, raster assets, font substitutions, missing references, and checks that were not performed.

Before plotting, confirm output formats (PNG, SVG, PDF, PPTX, or a combination)
and, for complex combined figures, whether external icon fetching is wanted.
Honor choices already explicit for the figure; otherwise wait for answers before
fetching icons, composing, or exporting. Source inspection may continue while
waiting. Record the choices and deliver the selected formats.

When PPTX is requested, use native text, shapes, and connectors; disclose any
picture or outlined-equation regions. Inspect a render from the PPTX itself,
report edit/save/reopen evidence, and preserve manual PowerPoint edits before
regeneration. A whole-figure image on a slide is not an editable diagram.
